About Our Company

Care for Trees. Respect for Nature.

Tree Bees Arboriculture LLC is a tree-care and arborist services company based in Glenrock, Wyoming. Our service focus encompasses tree maintenance, tree health, landscape preservation, and practical solutions for tree-related concerns.

We recognize the important role trees play in the character, comfort, and environmental value of outdoor spaces. Our range of services is intended to help property owners make informed decisions about maintaining and managing their trees.

  • Tree maintenance and pruning services
  • Tree health and structural care considerations
  • Tree removal and cleanup services
  • Planting and landscape preservation
