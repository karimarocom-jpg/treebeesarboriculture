Privacy Policy Overview

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Tree Bees Arboriculture LLC ("Tree Bees," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you visit our website or use a mobile application published by our organization.

This policy contains two main sections: our Website Privacy Policy, which covers our website, and our Mobile Apps Privacy Policy, which describes our stated data practices for our mobile applications.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through our website or mobile applications as described in this policy. If you voluntarily contact us by email or telephone, we may receive information you choose to provide through that communication.

This policy should be read together with the actual features and behavior of the website and any mobile application associated with Tree Bees Arboriculture LLC.

Section 01

Website Privacy Policy

This section applies to visitors of the Tree Bees Arboriculture LLC website and explains our approach to information collection, use, cookies, and communication.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is intended to provide information about our company, tree-care services, and business contact details. The website does not provide an account registration system or a website contact form that submits information to our servers.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through the website. If you choose to contact us using your email application or by telephone, you provide information directly through the communication method you select.

2. How We Use Information

Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us may be used to respond to your inquiry, discuss our services, and communicate with you about your request.

We do not sell personal information or use website-submitted personal information for advertising purposes.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website is designed without advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels implemented by Tree Bees Arboriculture LLC. Your hosting provider or other infrastructure providers may independently process technical connection information, such as server logs, to operate, protect, and maintain the website.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of hosting providers or your browser, email provider, or telephone carrier.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website provides links and contact methods that allow you to navigate to other services or contact us through your own email application or telephone service. Interactions with third-party services are subject to the privacy policies and practices of those providers.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take a reasonable, proportionate approach to protecting information that we receive through voluntary communications. Such information may be retained as necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

No method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended to provide general business information and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

7. Changes to This Website Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our website, business practices, or applicable legal requirements. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date when appropriate.

Section 02

Mobile Apps Privacy Policy

This section describes the privacy practices of mobile applications published by Tree Bees Arboriculture LLC that are covered by this policy.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Our mobile applications are designed not to collect, request, transmit, or store users' personal information. They do not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other identifying information to use their core functionality.

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information because our applications do not intentionally collect personal information as described in this section.

2. No Tracking or Analytics

Our mobile applications do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking technologies to track users' activities.

We do not use our mobile applications to build advertising profiles or track users across other applications or websites.

3. Permissions and Device Information

Our applications are intended to request only the device permissions necessary for their advertised functionality, if any. We do not intentionally use device permissions to collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

The applications covered by this policy are designed without third-party advertising or analytics services. If a particular application includes a third-party component, platform service, or feature that changes its data practices, those practices must be reviewed and this policy updated as appropriate.

The Google Play Store and the Android operating system are independently operated services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies and settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Because the applications described here are designed not to collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain a separate user personal-information database for those applications. Any information a user independently shares with us outside an application, such as through email, is handled as described in the Website Privacy Policy and applicable law.

6. Children's Privacy

Our mobile applications are not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the applications covered by this policy.

7. Changes to This Mobile Apps Policy

We may revise this section if our applications or their data practices change. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of this policy, including a revised effective date where appropriate.

Important: The statements in this section must accurately describe every published application covered by this policy. If an application uses analytics, advertising SDKs, account systems, or collects personal or device data, revise this policy before publishing it.
